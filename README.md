# Liberty or Death - Hearts of Iron IV Total Conversion

This repository contains a Hearts of Iron IV total conversion mod that shifts the grand strategy sandbox to the era of the American Revolutionary War.

## Features
- **New Start Date**: Campaign begins on 19 April 1775 with a truncated timeframe focused on the War of Independence.
- **Playable Factions**: Continental Congress (USA), British Empire (GBR), and Kingdom of France (FRA). Other tags are locked to a neutral status.
- **Overhauled Technology Tree**: Research lines cover colonial infantry, logistics, artillery, industry, governance, and age-of-sail naval doctrines built specifically for the 1770s.
- **Expanded National Focus Trees**: Dozens of bespoke focuses per playable nation drive military, diplomatic, political, and narrative paths, including historical and divergent outcomes.
- **Decisions and Events**: Dynamic decisions and a suite of scripted events narrate the march toward independence, diplomatic maneuvering, and imperial responses.
- **Order of Battle Overhaul**: New division templates and starting armies modeled after historical formations and colonial militias.
- **Full Japanese Localisation**: All custom content—focuses, events, technologies, ideas, and decisions—ships with native Japanese text and assumes all DLC features are available.

## Structure
The mod files live inside the `american_revolution` directory with a companion `american_revolution.mod` descriptor for use with the HOI4 launcher.

## Usage
Copy the `american_revolution` folder and the `american_revolution.mod` descriptor into your HOI4 user mod directory (typically `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod`). Activate "Liberty or Death" in the launcher to play.
